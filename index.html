About Sharshal Enterprises

Sharshal Enterprises is a proudly Zimbabwean business consultancy firm, incorporated in 2019 and wholly owned by local entrepreneurs. We are built on a deep understanding of the local business landscape and a commitment to empowering enterprises of all sizes.

We provide integrated, end-to-end business support services designed to ensure regulatory compliance, enhance financial health, and streamline operations. Our mission is to liberate business owners from the complexities of administration, allowing them to focus on their core objectives and strategic growth.

Vision

To be the leading and most trusted Zimbabwean-owned business consultancy, recognized for our integrity, expertise, and role as a catalyst for local business prosperity.

Mission

To deliver exceptional, reliable, and tailored business support services that guarantee compliance, optimize financial performance, and contribute to the sustainable success of our clients.

Our Services

Corporate Secretarial Services

Ensuring your company meets all its legal and statutory obligations with the Companies Registry.

  • Company Registration & De-registration
  • Preparation & Submission of Annual Returns
  • Share Capital Management (Increases & Decreases)
  • Share Transfers & Allotments
  • Changes in Directorship & Company Secretary

Taxation Advisory & Compliance

Navigating the ZIMRA landscape to ensure accurate submissions and optimal tax positions.

  • ZIMRA TIN Registrations
  • Preparation and Submission of Monthly & Quarterly Tax Returns (PAYE, VAT, CIT, etc.)
  • Application & Renewal of Tax Clearance Certificates
  • Tax Advisory and Planning

Accounting, Audit & Payroll Services

Providing clarity and insight into your financial position for informed decision-making.

  • Payroll Management: End-to-end payroll processing, PAYE computations, and payroll reporting
